Guava Cream Cheese Pastry

Cuban Breakfast Pastry

Puff Pastry Dough Ingredients

2 cups all-purpose flour
1 tsp salt
2.5 sticks cold unsalted butter
1/2 cup cold water

Filling Ingredients

2 tbsp warm water
5-6 oz (1/2 a package) of guava paste
1 egg
2-3 tbsp granulated sugar

Making the puff pastry dough

1. Mix the flour and salt in a bowl.
2. Cut the cold butter into thin slices and coat them in the flour mixture.
3. Add the cold water until the dough starts forming.
4. Gather the dough in a ball and wrap it with plastic wrap. Chill in the refridgerator for 1 hour.
5. Unwrap the dough, flour a surface, and use a rolling pin to flatten the ball.
6. Fold the flattened dough on itself in thirds and flatten at a 90 degree angle with the rolling pin.
7. Repeat this 2-4 times.
8. Wrap it in plastic and chill overnight.

Making the pastry

1. Unroll the dough and flatten until roughly 1/8" high.
2. Cut the dough into 2-inch squares.
3. Place one tsp of cream cheese and guava on top of each other in the center of the square.
4. Place another 2-inch square on top of the first square. Use a fork to push the edges together and crimp them.
5. Score the surface of one side of the pastry twice. This will release steam while the pastry is cooking.
6. Beat an egg in a bowl. Brush the surface of the pastry with egg wash and sprinkle with raw sugar.
7. Bake in an oven at 400F for 20 minutes.
7. Cool for 15 minutes before serving.
