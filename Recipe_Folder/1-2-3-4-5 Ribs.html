Chinese Braised Spare Ribs

Ingredients

Pork Ribs
1 tbsp shaoxing wine
2 tbsp dark soy sauce
3 tbsp rice vinegar
4 tbsp brown sugar
5 tbsp water

Instructions

1. Cut the pork ribs into 1 inch chunks. Brine the pork in salt water overnight.
2. Remove the ribs from the fridge to warm up before cooking. Cooking cold pork makes it hard and dry.
3. Add all ingredients to a pot on high heat and simmer for 40 minutes on low heat.
4. After cooking, turn up the heat to boil off the water.
