Steamed Spare Ribs

Steamed Spare Ribs

Ingredients

Spare Ribs
Scallions
Red Chili Peppers
Vegetable Oil

Marinade

Salt
White Sugar
Light Soy Sauce
Oyster Sauce
Shaoxing Wine
Garlic
Ginger
Dried Mandarin Orange Peel
Dried Fermented Black Bean
Sesame Oil

Instructions

1. Rehydrate fermented black beans and dried mandarin peels for 5-10 minutes.
2. Chop spare ribs into 1-1.5 inch chunks; separate the bone chunks from each other.
2a. Optional: Massage ribs with cornstarch to bind the myoglobin and rinse it off under water.
3. Massage ribs with marinade and let rest overnight.
4. Spread ribs evenly on a shallow bowl. Prevent any ribs from covering each other.
5. Add chili pepper slices and a little vegetable oil on the surface of the ribs to provide a better finish.
5. Add water under a steaming rack and steam on high heat for 20 minutes.
6. Garnish with scallions after the ribs are done cooking.
