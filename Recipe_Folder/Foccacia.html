Foccacia

Italian Flatbread

Biga

2.25 cup bread flour
1/3 tsp yeast
2/3 room temp water

Dough

1 tsp yeast
1 + 0.33 cup warm water
2.25 cups bread flour
2.5 tsp salt
Olive Oil
Rosemary
Thinly sliced garlic

Prepare the biga

1. Mix biga ingredients and let rest for 24 hours. Don't overfold. It should be able to form a ball, but should still be a little sticky.

Making the dough

1. Mix the flour and 1 cup of water. Let rest for 30 minutes to autolyse.
2. Add the biga, yeast, salt, 0.33 cup water and mix until uniform.
3. Let the dough rest for 30 minutes
4. Stretch and Fold: Pick up the dough from the top and fold towards the bottom of the dough. Turn 90 degrees and repeat 8 times. Cover and rest again for 30 minutes.
5. Repeat the Stretch and Fold step 4 more times, resting 30 minutes between each time.
6. Transfer the dough to a pan, cover and let rest for an hour for air bubbles to form. It should spread across the pan, not rise up during this process.
7. Press down and stretch the dough into each corner. Dimple the surface and drizzle olive oil over the surface of the dough without deflating air bubbles.
8. Season surface with salt, rosemary, and garlic
8. Bake at 425F for 20 minutes.

Notes

09/02/2026: The texture and appearance looked good. But the bread was very tough to tear apart and was a little like leather after I let it sit overnight in the oven. Maybe too much water content. Bottom of foccacia burned black. Add extra sheet under the bread to shield it from direct heat.
