Flatbread

Flatbread

Ingredients

4 cups All-Purpose Flour
2 tsp Yeast
2 tsp Kosher Salt
1.5 cups Warm Water
2 tsp granulated sugar
2 tbsp Olive Oil

Instructions

1. Mix the yeast, sugar and warm water. Let sit for 5 minutes to active.
2. Combine the flour and salt. Mix in the activated yeast.
3. After the dough is formed, add olive oil and combine.
4. Allow dough to proof for a few hours.
5. Roll out the dough and flatten into hand-shaped sizes. Fold the dough a few times to create layers.
6. Cook on the stovetop at high heat without oil. The air layers should expand and puff up.

Notes
