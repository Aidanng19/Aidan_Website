Burnt Ends

Southern pork belly recipe

Ingredients

Pork Belly
BBQ Sauce
Honey

Marinade

Salt
Pepper
Paprika
Chili Powder
Brown Sugar
Onion Powder

Instructions

1. Marinade the pork belly using the dry marinade ingredients for 24 hours.
2. Mix BBQ sauce and honey in a separate bowl.
3. Place pork belly on baking sheet, fat side up. Brush sauce on the side facing up.
4. Bake at 250F for 1.5 hours.
5. Flip the pork belly brush sauce again and bake for another 1.5 hours.
6. Let the meat rest in the residue heat.
